How music folders work

Pick a folder, not files. Choose one or more directories that contain your albums — Music, Audio, a USB tree. Everything inside is scanned recursively.

Your library stays. Roots and the catalog are saved with Android's persistable permissions, so the same music is still here the next time you open the app.

Files stay put. Removing a folder from the library never deletes anything on your device.
